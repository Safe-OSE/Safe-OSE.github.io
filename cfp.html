Call for Papers

SafeOSE invites original research on security, privacy, safety, and trust in open-source and standards-driven ecosystems. The research-paper program is separate from the workshop's two-track hackathon.

Research scope

Topics of interest

We welcome technical, empirical, interdisciplinary, and community-centered work that studies how vulnerabilities and defenses propagate through shared artifacts, standards, governance structures, communities, and dependencies. Standardized IoT and AI-agent ecosystems are prominent examples, but the research scope extends across open-source and standards-driven ecosystems in all domains.

  • Security and privacy of open-source ecosystems
  • Analysis of open standards, specifications, protocols, and reference implementations
  • Open-source AI and agent ecosystems: NLIP, A2A, MCP, AGNTCY, AG2, NANDA, and more
  • Standardized IoT: Matter, MQTT, AMQP, CoAP, BLE, and their real-world deployment
  • Agent identity, authorization, delegation, accountability, and information flow in OSE
  • SBOM, CBOM, AI-BOM, and provenance in open-source ecosystems
  • Verification, program analysis, fuzzing, and AI-assisted analysis
  • Trust, privacy, accountability, policy, regulation, and incentives
  • Ecosystem governance, maintainers, disclosure, and sustainability
  • Measurements, benchmarks, datasets, adoption, and case studies

Paper submissions

Submission information

SafeOSE accepts regular papers of up to 8 pages and short or work-in-progress papers of up to 4 pages. Submissions must use the NDSS workshop proceedings format and will undergo double-blind peer review.

Accepted papers will be published in official workshop proceedings through the Internet Society. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present at the workshop.
