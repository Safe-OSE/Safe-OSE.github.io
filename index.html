Why SafeOSE

Think beyond the repository.

An open-source ecosystem is more than code or a dependency graph. It includes shared standards, protocols, infrastructure, governance, contributor communities, downstream adopters, and the trust relationships connecting them. A weakness in any shared foundation can propagate across products, organizations, applications, and users.

01

Shared foundations

Study standards, protocols, reference implementations, platforms, and infrastructure whose security properties travel downstream.

02

Human ecosystems

Examine maintainers, contributors, users, governance, disclosure, incentives, sustainability, and community processes.

03

Collective resilience

Develop interventions that strengthen whole ecosystems—not only isolated components—across software, IoT, cloud, and AI.

Our lineage

SafeOSE grows from SDIoTSec.

SafeOSE is the natural evolution of the NDSS Workshop on Security and Privacy in Standardized IoT, held annually since 2024. It preserves that community and ecosystem-level perspective while expanding beyond IoT to open-source infrastructure, software platforms, AI systems, and agent ecosystems.
